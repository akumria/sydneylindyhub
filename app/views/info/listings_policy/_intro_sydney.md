# Listings policy

Sydney Lindy Hub lists recurring classes and social dances that are useful to
the local swing dance community. Listings are curated by volunteers from public
organiser sources and record when an editor last verified them.

To suggest an event, email
[hello@sydneylindyhub.au](mailto:hello@sydneylindyhub.au?subject=Event%20listing)
with the organiser's public source URL, venue, dates, and any known
cancellations. Please allow time for a volunteer to check the details.
