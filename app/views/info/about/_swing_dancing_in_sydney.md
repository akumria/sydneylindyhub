Swing dancing in Sydney
-----------------------

Sydney's scene includes Lindy Hop, Balboa, Collegiate Shag, solo jazz, and
related dances. Events range from weekly classes and social nights to live-band
dances and occasional workshops.

### Finding current information

Organiser pages remain the authoritative source because venues, schedules, and
ticket arrangements can change. The public
[Sydney Let's Swing Dance Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1179196565445087)
is also a useful place to ask local dancers for current advice.
